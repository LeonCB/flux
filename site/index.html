In Flux, choose which display shows the clock. Always top center. Always optional.

01

Every screen, its own flow.

Independent animation on each display, with its own resolution and scaling. One key or mouse movement brings you back.

02

Set your own pace.

Adjust speed, line size, and simulation quality. Your preferences stay with you through updates.

03

Time, when you want it.

A quiet 24-hour clock, centered at the top of your chosen display. Turn it on or leave the light alone.

Get Flux ↓
  1. 1

    Make a little room.

    Choose MSI or ZIP on the GitHub release page. Close Flux and Windows Screen Saver Settings before installation or update.

  2. 2

    Install just for you.

    Run the MSI, or extract the ZIP and run Install.cmd. No administrator rights needed. Open Windows Screen Saver Settings, check your wait time and sign-in preference, then click Apply.

  3. 3

    Choose your atmosphere.

    Open Settings to pick a palette, tune the motion, and enable the clock. Changes apply on the next launch.

MSI uninstall note: after a failed uninstall and rollback, a later removal can report success while leaving files. Reinstall/repair with the same MSI, then retry removal; this recovery passed the tests. See the release notes and recovery details. The old Inno EXE is withdrawn following antivirus detections.
